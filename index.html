Dr. Honey Walrond

I am an Assistant Professor of Educational Leadership in the Department of Educational Policy Studies at Georgia State University. Prior to that, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar at the University of California, Berkeley, School of Education. I use mixed and qualitative research methods to illuminate 1) how Black leaders create affirming learning environments for Black students and 2) how leaders engage Black families and communities in shared governance for school- and district-level decision-making. Drawing on qualitative and mixed-methods research, my scholarship has been published in Urban Education, Leadership and Policy in Schools, Journal of Educational Supervision, and the Journal of Black Studies. I earned my Ph.D. in English (Literacy) Education and Educational Leadership and Policy from Columbia University as a Provost Doctoral Fellow.
